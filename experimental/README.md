# Experimental

A sandbox for recipe ideas, ingredient combinations, weird flavor pairings, and food experiments Emma wants to play with — things that haven't earned a spot in `preferences.md` yet.

## Rules for this folder

**Opt-in only.** This folder is NOT loaded by default. It should be ignored by:

- The weekly meal-plan Routine
- Default weekly meal planning, grocery lists, or "what's for dinner this week" suggestions
- Anything that's just a normal meal-planning task

**Read this folder only when:**

- Emma explicitly asks for new recipe brainstorming ("look in experimental", "any ideas from the experimental folder", etc.)
- Emma is exploring "what could I do with X ingredient"
- It's a quiet weekend and she wants to mine for inspiration
- A task specifically calls for unvetted/exploratory ideas

## What lives here

Items in this folder are explicitly **unvetted**. They may stretch or even cross her usual constraints (kidney stones, insulin-conscious, etc.) — that's part of the point. The folder is meant to be a playground, not a curated list.

Things that prove out — that she actually tries and enjoys — get promoted to `preferences.md` (Loved recipes or Things to try).

## Structure

```
experimental/
├── README.md            ← you are here
├── flavor-matrix/       ← structured flavor-pairing data; see its own README
└── recipes/             ← one .md file per experimental recipe sketch
```

### Recipes

Each recipe page in `recipes/` follows a loose convention: a Status line ("Not yet tried" / "Tried once" / "Made multiple times"), an Inspiration note (where the idea came from — flavor matrix, friend, cookbook, etc.), Quick stats, Ingredients, Method, Constraint notes (kidney-stone / sodium / oxalate flags), Variations, and a blank "Notes after cooking" section to fill in after the first attempt.

When a recipe earns its keep, copy a one-line summary to `preferences.md` → Loved recipes and leave the detailed page here (or delete it, your call).
