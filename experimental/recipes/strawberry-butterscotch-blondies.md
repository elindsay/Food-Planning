# Strawberry Butterscotch Blondies — two variants

**Status:** Not yet tried
**Inspiration:** Flavor-matrix exploration. Strawberry and butterscotch share **furaneol at HIGH strength on both sides** — furaneol is the molecule that makes a strawberry smell like a strawberry, and it's one of the three defining compounds in butterscotch. Same flavor family, completely different cultural associations, which is why "strawberry blondie with butterscotch chips" sounds weird but tastes uncannily right. White chocolate bridges via vanillin + creamy lactones.

Two variants below — same flavor concept, different visual outcomes depending on whether you want the strawberry to *look* pink or just *smell* like strawberry.

## Quick stats

- **Active time:** ~15 min
- **Total time:** ~50 min
- **Dishes:** 1 saucepan (or microwave-safe bowl) + 1 mixing bowl + 1 baking pan
- **Yields:** one 8x8 or 9x9 pan, ~16 bars

## Shared ingredients (both variants)

- 2 large eggs + 1 yolk
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup freeze-dried strawberries, crushed to a coarse powder (Trader Joe's and Whole Foods both carry them; Target sometimes too)
- 3/4 cup butterscotch chips
- 1/2 cup white chocolate chips
- *Optional:* flaky sea salt + a few extra crushed freeze-dried strawberries for the top

## Variant 1: Rosy 🌸 (pink-leaning)

**For when you want the blondies to *look* like they have strawberry in them.**

Swap-in ingredients:

- 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, **melted** (do not brown — keeps the color competition low)
- 1 1/4 cups **white sugar** + 1/4 cup light brown sugar (mostly white sugar = less competing color, more rosy crumb)

**What you'll see:** distinctly pink-rosy raw batter (almost startling the first time). Baked crumb is a sunset-rose color with deeper red flecks throughout. Crust still browns golden-tan on top, but the inside reads visibly pink. Pretty, intentional-looking.

## Variant 2: Moody 🌒 (aromatic, less pink)

**For when you want maximum strawberry flavor depth without the visual loudness.**

Swap-in ingredients:

- 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, **browned** then cooled 5 min
- 1 1/2 cups packed **dark brown sugar** (deep caramelization = the strawberry-furaneol layer reads as more aromatic, less visual)

**What you'll see:** tawny-brown blondies with subtle pink-red flecks throughout, like good shortbread with hidden surprises. Smells intensely of strawberry-caramel when warm. The flavor is fuller and more complex than the rosy variant — brown butter + dark brown sugar amplify the furaneol overlap with butterscotch more than the rosy version does.

## Method (same for both variants)

1. **Preheat** oven to 350°F. Line an 8x8 or 9x9 pan with parchment, leaving overhang on two sides as handles.

2. **Prepare the butter** per your chosen variant:
   - *Rosy:* Melt butter gently in a saucepan or microwave-safe bowl. Let cool 5 min.
   - *Moody:* Melt butter in a light-colored saucepan over medium heat, swirling, until milk solids turn deep golden-brown and smell nutty (~5–7 min). Pour into a large bowl (scrape the brown bits) and cool 5 min.

3. **Whisk in sugar(s)** until smooth and glossy. Mixture will look like wet sand.

4. **Whisk in eggs, yolk, and vanilla** until fully combined.

5. **Stir in flour, baking powder, salt, and crushed freeze-dried strawberries** with a spatula until no dry streaks remain. Don't overmix.

6. **Fold in butterscotch and white chocolate chips.** Reserve a small handful of each for the top if you like.

7. **Spread evenly** in the pan. Scatter reserved chips on top. For the rosy variant, sprinkle a few more crushed strawberry bits on top for visual pop. Add a pinch of flaky salt.

8. **Bake** 25–30 min, until edges are set and the center is just set with a faint jiggle (toothpick: few moist crumbs, not wet batter). Underbaked > overbaked.

9. **Cool at least 30 min** in the pan before lifting out by the parchment handles and cutting.

## Constraint notes (Emma-specific)

- **Oxalate:** chocolate chips are moderate-oxalate; one or two bars per sitting is fine. White chocolate is lower than dark. Strawberries are very low oxalate.
- **Sodium:** standard — about 50 mg per bar from the added salt + chips. Comfortably within budget.
- **No nuts**, no high-purine ingredients.
- **Freeze-dried over fresh:** fresh strawberries water down the batter and dilute the furaneol. Freeze-dried concentrates the strawberry flavor and avoids the moisture problem. Don't substitute.

## Variations

- **Real strawberry jam swirl:** add 2–3 tbsp strawberry jam dolloped over the batter before baking; swirl with a knife. Works with either variant. Adds visual pink streaks.
- **No white chocolate:** double the butterscotch chips for a more intensely caramel-forward bar.
- **Vanilla amp:** add the seeds from half a vanilla bean (or 1 tsp vanilla bean paste) to the wet ingredients — vanillin reinforces both the strawberry-furaneol and butterscotch profiles.
- **Add freeze-dried raspberry instead** for a slightly different floral profile (raspberry has the β-ionone violet compound instead of strawberry's straight furaneol). Could even do half-and-half.
