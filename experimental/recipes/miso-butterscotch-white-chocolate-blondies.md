# Miso Butterscotch White Chocolate Blondies

**Status:** Not yet tried
**Inspiration:** Flavor-matrix exploration. Butterscotch and white miso share three compounds at meaningful strength (furaneol, 3-methylbutanal, 2,5-dimethylpyrazine — the caramelized + malty + roasty trio). White chocolate bridges via vanillin + diacetyl. Reads on paper as "miso in a dessert?" but the chemistry says it's just an unusually well-stocked salted-caramel flavor stack.

## Quick stats

- **Active time:** ~20 min
- **Total time:** ~55 min (incl. baking + brief cool)
- **Dishes:** 1 saucepan + 1 bowl + 1 baking pan
- **Yields:** one 8x8 or 9x9 pan, ~16 bars
- **Sodium:** miso adds ~700–900 mg total across the whole pan = ~50 mg per bar. Comfortably within budget.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter
- 1 1/2 cups packed light or dark brown sugar
- 2 large eggs + 1 yolk (the extra yolk gives chewy-fudgy texture)
- 2 tbsp white miso (shiro miso)
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt *(reduced from the usual 3/4 tsp because the miso adds saltiness)*
- 3/4 cup butterscotch chips
- 1/2 cup white chocolate chips
- *Optional:* flaky sea salt for topping

## Method

1. **Preheat** oven to 350°F. Line an 8x8 or 9x9 pan with parchment, leaving overhang on two sides as handles.

2. **Brown the butter.** Melt butter in a light-colored saucepan over medium heat. Continue cooking, swirling occasionally, until the milk solids turn deep golden-brown and smell nutty — about 5–7 min. Pour into a large bowl (scrape the brown bits — they're the flavor) and let cool 5 min until just warm, not hot.

3. **Whisk in brown sugar** until smooth and glossy. Mixture will look like wet sand.

4. **Whisk in the white miso, eggs, yolk, and vanilla** until fully combined. The miso dissolves into the wet ingredients seamlessly — no visible miso in the final batter.

5. **Stir in flour, baking powder, and salt** with a spatula just until no dry streaks remain. Don't overmix.

6. **Fold in butterscotch chips and white chocolate chips.** Reserve a small handful of each for the top if you want them visible.

7. **Spread evenly** in the prepared pan. Scatter the reserved chips on top. Sprinkle with a pinch of flaky salt.

8. **Bake** 25–30 min, until the edges are golden and the center is just set with a faint jiggle (a toothpick should come out with a few moist crumbs, not wet batter). Underbaked is better than overbaked for blondies.

9. **Cool at least 30 min** in the pan before lifting out by the parchment handles and cutting. Sprinkle with more flaky salt right before serving if desired.

## Constraint notes (Emma-specific)

- **Sodium:** 2 tbsp white miso = ~700 mg sodium spread across 16 bars = ~45 mg per bar. The reduced added salt (1/2 tsp vs 3/4 tsp) keeps total in check. One or two bars fits comfortably in your daily budget.
- **Oxalate:** chocolate chips have moderate oxalate; one or two bars per sitting is fine. White chocolate is lower-oxalate than dark.
- **No high-oxalate nuts**, no high-purine ingredients.
- **Insulin:** these are dessert. They are sweet. Eat them in joyful moderation — don't kid yourself that the miso makes them health food.

## Variations

- **No white chocolate:** double the butterscotch chips (1 1/4 cups total) for a more intensely butterscotch-forward bar.
- **Salt-caramel push:** drizzle 2 tbsp caramel sauce over the batter before baking and swirl with a knife.
- **Espresso amp:** add 2 tsp instant espresso powder with the dry ingredients — coffee shares 4 compounds with butterscotch and adds depth (doesn't make them taste like coffee).
- **Smaller batch:** halve everything, bake in an 8x4 loaf pan for ~20 min.
- **Tahini variant:** swap miso for 2 tbsp tahini — different umami pathway (sesame Maillard compounds), well-trodden chef move.
