# Emma's Food Preferences

A living document — append to this as new preferences, favorites, or dislikes come up. Read alongside `CLAUDE.md` before suggesting meals or recipes.

## Foods Emma doesn't like

- **Zucchini** — sub with cherry tomatoes, asparagus, snap peas, or bell pepper depending on the dish.

## Foods Emma uses sparingly

- **Tuna** — not a flavor favorite, and mercury is a concern with frequent canned tuna. Occasionally is fine; don't lean on it as a workhorse fish. When a vintage/Mediterranean recipe calls for tuna, swap to canned salmon, cooked shrimp, or chickpeas depending on the dish.

## Cooking notes

- **Cooking chicken feels annoying.** Minimize chicken in meal plans. Default protein workhorses are: fish (salmon, cod, white fish, shrimp), tofu, beans/lentils, eggs. Use chicken only when there's a specific reason it earns its place.

## Dairy preferences

- **Full-fat Greek yogurt preferred** over 2% or 0%. Aligns with the insulin-conscious / obesity-code approach in CLAUDE.md and is fine for kidney-stone goals (fat content doesn't affect stone risk; calcium content is similar across fat levels).

## Pantry & brand notes

- **Protein powder: Sunwarrior plant-based (pea + brown rice + pumpkin seed blend).** Emma uses **one scoop only** — two scoops is unpleasant to drink. Typically blended with frozen fruit (berries, mango), occasionally a little kale, no added sweeteners. One scoop = ~15g protein and ~180 mg sodium, so count the sodium toward the daily ~2,300 mg target on shake days. If a shake is the main protein for a meal (e.g., post-workout), boost it with Greek yogurt, hemp seeds, or nut butter to get closer to 25g protein.

## Loved recipes

- **Deviled eggs** (paprika, dill, a little Dijon) — pre-existing favorite. Great fridge snack and an effortless way to use hard-boiled eggs from a batch. Pure 60s vibe.
- **Shrimp cocktail** (chilled shrimp + horseradish-tomato sauce + lemon) — pre-existing favorite. Steakhouse/70s-coded, naturally low effort if you buy cooked shrimp.
- **Coq au vin** — tried once in a cooking class and enjoyed it. Worth revisiting but not a regular thing; it's a project meal.

## Things Emma wants to learn / try

### 60s/70s vibe — weeknight-friendly

- **Waldorf salad** — apple, celery, walnuts, raisins, with Greek yogurt subbed for mayo.
- **Cottage cheese plate** — full-fat cottage cheese with peach halves or pineapple, cucumber, tomato, paprika. Mid-century lunch aesthetic.
- **Salmon patties** — canned salmon, egg, breadcrumb, lemon zest, dill, pan-fried in butter. Classic 60s pantry meal, very fast.
- **Stuffed bell peppers** — bell peppers stuffed with lentils, brown rice, tomato, herbs, baked under a little cheese. Vegetarian update on the era's beef-and-rice version.
- **Mushroom-barley soup** — onions, carrots, celery, mushrooms, pearl barley, broth, dill. Hands-off simmer; reheats well for lunches.

### 60s/70s vibe — weekend / project meals

These are the more involved or special-feeling ones — save for unhurried evenings.

- **Quiche** — eggs, cream, leeks, mushrooms, gruyère, in a whole-wheat crust (or crustless). Julia Child energy.
- **Cheese fondue** — gruyère + white wine + garlic + cubed bread, broccoli, apples, blanched cauliflower for dipping. Friday-night ritual.
- **Salmon en papillote** — salmon in a parchment packet with leeks, lemon, herbs, butter. Looks fancy, 20 min.
- **Trout almondine** — pan-fried trout with brown butter and toasted almonds. Simple but feels like a restaurant dish.
- **Mushroom stroganoff** — sautéed mushrooms, onions, sour cream, broth, smoked paprika over egg noodles. Vegetarian cousin of beef stroganoff.
- **Vichyssoise** — cold leek and potato soup with a swirl of cream. Needs chill time, so plan ahead. Summer-friendly.

### 60s/70s vibe — maybe occasionally (B-list, open to but lower priority)

- **Cobb salad** — composed-salad pageantry: chicken, egg, avocado, blue cheese, tomato, romaine.
- **Stuffed mushrooms** — button mushrooms with breadcrumb-parmesan-herb filling. Cocktail party classic.
- **Niçoise salad** — French-housewife elegance with eggs, green beans, olives, potatoes. (Traditionally tuna; swap to canned salmon per the tuna note above.)
- **Tuna noodle casserole, made from scratch** — also a candidate for salmon substitution; otherwise saved for when canned-tuna-occasionally is in budget.

### Vegetarian & vegan workhorses — weeknight-friendly (≤30 min, mostly one pan/pot)

A curated set of high-protein vegetarian/vegan dishes pulled from across cuisines. Tempeh- and miso-leaning where possible; spinach swapped for kale/chard everywhere.

- **Cottage cheese savory bowl** — full-fat cottage cheese, cucumber, cherry tomato, olive oil, dill, optional soft-boiled egg on top. 5 minutes, ~30g protein.
- **Avocado toast with cottage cheese underneath** — layer cottage cheese on the toast first, then mashed avocado, sea salt, chili flakes. ~25g protein.
- **White bean "tuna" salad** — mashed cannellini + celery + red onion + capers + lemon + Dijon + olive oil (or Greek yogurt). **Great as a sandwich** with lettuce and tomato on whole-wheat bread, or scooped onto greens. Fridge 3 days; doesn't freeze.
- **Greek lentil + feta salad** — pre-cooked French/green lentils + cucumber + tomato + red onion + parsley + feta + lemon-olive oil. ~25g protein. Holds in fridge 4 days.
- **Shakshuka** — eggs poached in spiced tomato sauce with feta. One pan, ~25g protein.
- **Red lentil dal (tadka)** — onion + garlic + ginger + tomato + red lentils + coconut milk + garam masala + cilantro. ~25 min one-pot. **Freezes great.** ~20g protein.
- **Miso-glazed tempeh** — cubed tempeh pan-seared, glazed with white miso + mirin + a touch of maple + ginger + sesame oil. Serve over rice with broccoli. ~25g protein. Tempeh + miso double-win.
- **Tempeh stir-fry** — tempeh + broccoli + bell peppers + ginger-garlic-low-sodium-tamari over rice or soba noodles. ~25g protein.
- **Build-a-bowl: brown rice + miso soup + edamame + sesame-marinated tofu** — Japanese basics, modular. Skip salty pickles to keep sodium reasonable.
- **Pasta e fagioli** — Italian pasta-and-bean soup with cannellini, tomato, rosemary, small pasta. **Freezes well.** Comfort food, fast.
- **Coconut chickpea curry with cauliflower & kale** — chickpeas + cauliflower + green beans + kale + coconut milk + curry powder. **Freezes great**, big batch.
- **Crustless quiche / frittata variations** — leeks + goat cheese, mushrooms + gruyère, broccoli + cheddar. Fridge 4 days; doesn't freeze as well but reheats nicely.

### Vegetarian & vegan workhorses — weekend / slightly more involved (mostly hands-off simmering)

- **Mujadara** — lentils + rice + deeply caramelized onions + cumin. Lebanese pantry staple. Caramelizing onions is the time sink; rest is hands-off. **Freezes well.**
- **Chana saag with kale** — chickpeas in a creamy kale-based curry. **Freezes great**, eats three days.
- **Greek gigantes plaki** — large white beans baked in tomato sauce with dill, feta on top. Mostly oven time. **Freezes well.**
- **White bean & kale soup with parmesan rind** — Tuscan-style. Cannellini, kale, broth, parm rind simmered in, lemon at the end. **Freezes great.**
- **Misir wat (Ethiopian red lentil stew)** — berbere-spiced, deep, smoky. Eaten with injera or rice. **Freezes well.** ~20g protein per serving.
- **Lentil-mushroom bolognese** — green or beluga lentils + finely chopped mushrooms + tomato + soffritto, over whole-wheat pasta. **Freezes great** as the sauce. Hearty, meat-like texture.
- **Sopa de frijoles negros (Cuban black bean soup)** — black beans + cumin + oregano + lime. Top with avocado, queso fresco, soft-boiled egg. **Freezes well.**
